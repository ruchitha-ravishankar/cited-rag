# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

## Benefit
Eligible farmer families receive income support of Rs 6,000 per year. The amount is paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 each, one every four months. Money is transferred directly into the beneficiary's bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Who is eligible
All landholding farmer families are eligible, subject to the exclusion criteria. A family means husband, wife and minor children. The scheme was first limited to small and marginal farmers and was later extended to all landholding farmer families.

## Exclusions
The following categories are not eligible:
- Institutional landholders
- Holders of constitutional posts and former holders of such posts
- Serving or retired officers and employees of central or state governments (multi-tasking staff and Group D employees are excepted)
- Persons who paid income tax in the last assessment year
- Professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers and chartered accountants who practise
- Pensioners receiving a monthly pension of Rs 10,000 or more

## Documents needed
Aadhaar number, bank account details and land records are needed for registration. Registration can be done through the PM-KISAN portal or a Common Service Centre.
