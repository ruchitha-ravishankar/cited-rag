# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY

## Health cover
The scheme provides health cover of Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. Treatment is cashless and paperless at empanelled public and private hospitals.

## Coverage features
There is no cap on family size or age of members. Pre-existing conditions are covered from the first day of enrolment. The package includes pre-hospitalisation and post-hospitalisation expenses and medicines.

## Who is eligible
Eligibility for rural families is based on deprivation criteria from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011. For urban areas, certain occupational categories such as ragpickers, domestic workers, street vendors and construction workers are covered. From 2024, all senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible irrespective of income.

## How to check eligibility
Beneficiaries can check their name on the scheme portal using a mobile number or ration card number, or visit an empanelled hospital's Ayushman Mitra help desk. A card is issued after identity verification.
