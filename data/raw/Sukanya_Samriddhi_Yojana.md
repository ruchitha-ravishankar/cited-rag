# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

## Who can open an account
An account can be opened in the name of a girl child below 10 years of age by her natural or legal guardian. Only one account is allowed per girl child, and a family can open accounts for a maximum of two girls (with exceptions for twins or triplets).

## Deposits
The minimum deposit is Rs 250 in a financial year and the maximum is Rs 1.5 lakh in a financial year. Deposits can be made for 15 years from the date of opening the account.

## Maturity
The account matures 21 years after the date of opening.

## Partial withdrawal
Up to 50 percent of the balance at the end of the preceding financial year can be withdrawn once the girl turns 18 or passes class 10, for higher education or marriage expenses.

## Tax benefit
Deposits qualify for deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, and interest and maturity amounts are exempt from tax under the old tax regime.
