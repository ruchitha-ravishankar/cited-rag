# Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

## Purpose
APY is a government-backed pension scheme mainly for workers in the unorganised sector. It guarantees a fixed monthly pension after the age of 60.

## Eligibility
Any Indian citizen between 18 and 40 years of age with a savings bank account or post office savings account can join. A mobile number is required. Income tax payers are not eligible to join from 1 October 2022.

## Pension amounts
The subscriber chooses a monthly pension of Rs 1,000, Rs 2,000, Rs 3,000, Rs 4,000 or Rs 5,000, starting at age 60. The monthly contribution depends on the chosen pension amount and the age of joining. Joining earlier means a lower contribution.

## Contribution period
Contributions are made until the subscriber turns 60, and are auto-debited from the bank account.

## After the subscriber's death
The spouse receives the same pension after the subscriber's death. After both pass away, the nominee receives the pension wealth accumulated till age 60.
