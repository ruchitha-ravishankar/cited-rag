# PM Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

## Objective
PMUY was launched in May 2016 to provide clean cooking fuel by giving LPG connections to women from poor households, reducing the use of firewood and cow dung cakes.

## Eligibility
The applicant must be an adult woman belonging to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household. There must be no existing LPG connection in the household from any oil marketing company. Beneficiary categories include SC/ST households, Antyodaya Anna Yojana beneficiaries, forest dwellers, tea garden workers and the most backward classes.

## Financial support
Under the original scheme, a financial assistance of Rs 1,600 per connection was given, so no security deposit was needed. Under Ujjwala 2.0, the first refill and a hotplate stove were also provided free of cost.

## Documents for migrants
Under Ujjwala 2.0, migrant families can submit a self-declaration as proof of address instead of local address documents.

## How to apply
Applicants can submit the KYC form and documents at the nearest LPG distributor or apply online through the portal.
